Questions people ask before they book

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, bring it to the call.

Does this cost me anything?+

No. Insurance carriers pay me when you enroll in a plan. You never pay me, and the plan costs the same whether you enroll with me or on your own.

Do you offer every plan in my area?+

No. We do not offer every plan available in your area. Any information we provide is limited to those plans we do offer in your area. Please contact Medicare.gov or 1-800-MEDICARE to get information on all of your options.

Open enrollment ended December 7. Is it too late?+

Not always. If you have a Medicare Advantage plan, you can make one change between January 1 and March 31, either to another Advantage plan or back to Original Medicare. Turning 65 has its own window. And you can apply for a Medicare Supplement any time of year, though outside your guaranteed windows most states allow the carrier to ask health questions. Book a call and I'll tell you which windows you have.

Should I pick Medicare Advantage or a Medicare Supplement?+

Medicare Advantage isn't always bad, and a Supplement isn't always better. It depends on your doctors, your medications, how much you travel, and how you feel about predictable costs versus paying as you go. I'll show you both side by side with your own numbers.

I'm still working at 65. Do I need to sign up?+

It depends on the size of your employer and your coverage. This is one of the most common questions I get, and it's worth a 20-minute call to get right so you don't end up with a late penalty.

What should I have ready for the call?+

Your doctors' names and your prescriptions with doses help the most. Your Medicare card is useful too. If you don't have any of it handy, that's fine. We'll build the list together.

Can my son or daughter join the call?+

Yes, and I love when they do. A lot of my clients come to me through a daughter or son helping a parent. Everyone on the call hears the same thing, which makes the decision easier.

Do you work in my state?+

I'm licensed in Arizona, California, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Michigan, Nevada, New Mexico, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, Texas, Virginia and Washington. Everything happens by phone or video, so it doesn't matter how far you are from me.

Will I get a bunch of sales calls after I book?+

No. Your information stays with me, and I only reach out about the appointment you booked.

Do you help with Part D drug plans?+

Yes. Part D plans pay agents nothing, and I review them anyway. A drug plan can be an expensive decision, and I'm not going to leave you on your own with it.

I'm not 65 yet. Can you still help?+

Yes. We help people of any age with accident, cancer, heart and stroke, hospital indemnity and life coverage, so the costs life throws at you are covered alongside your health plan.
